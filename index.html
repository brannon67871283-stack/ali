THE PROBLEM

When every second counts

📵

Can’t reach your phone

Hands are busy, trapped or injured.

🤫

Can’t speak safely

Speaking out loud can put you in danger.

😰

Can’t type under stress

Typing an address takes time you don’t have.

THE SOLUTION

One gesture. One alert.

Hold up 1, 2 or 3 fingers in front of any webcam. The system reads your hand, grabs your location and sends a photo alert to Telegram within seconds.

HOW IT WORKS

From hand to help

01

Camera

Live webcam stream straight into the browser.

02

Hand tracking

MediaPipe locates 21 landmarks on your hand every frame.

03

Gesture decoder

Counts raised fingers and confirms a 1.2 s steady hold.

04

Dispatch

GPS position and a globe snapshot are broadcast to several Telegram recipients at once.

THE CODES

Finger codes

1
☝️

Medical emergency

Ambulance needed immediately.

2
✌️

Security threat

A threat has been detected at the location.

3
🤟

Critical distress

Maximum urgency, immediate intervention.

4+
🖐️

Critical, all-hands

Full broadcast at the highest severity.

✊ Closed fist = rotate and zoom the globe with your hand.

SAFETY

Built to avoid false alarms

⏱️

Hold to confirm

Gesture must stay steady for 1.2 seconds.

🧊

Cooldown

10 seconds between alerts prevents spam.

🔊

Voice confirmation

An Arabic voice announces the alert type.

📍

Location fallback

If GPS is denied, a default location is used.

🖥️

Simulation mode

Mouse and keyboard demo when no camera is available.

🎯

Live feedback

Progress ring and finger counter on screen.
